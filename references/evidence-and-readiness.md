# Detailed Evidence Extension

Evidence statuses, absence evidence, and readiness verdict semantics are defined
once in `SKILL.md` and apply to every mode. Detailed mode additionally preserves
directional relationships, conflicts, unknowns, and scoped readiness blockers
in the fields required by its template and validator.

Summary v2 classifies every open item as `hard_blocker`, `open_design_decision`,
`deployment_value`, or `recommendation`. Only `hard_blocker` requires `추가 정보 필요`.
